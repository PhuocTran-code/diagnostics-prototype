Diagnostic services warehouse: medallion architecture

A pathology and radiology provider with two laboratory systems (lis_a, lis_b) and one radiology system (ris). Data moves through bronze, silver, graph and gold layers into dashboards. Source, silver, audit DDL and sample rows are in diagnostics_source_schemas.sql; the identity-resolution logic is in diagnostics_graph_mpi.cypher. The graph layer is a deterministic, scaled-down implementation of the graph-based master patient index recommended in Assignment 1 (R1).

The sample rows include one patient who appears in all three systems under three different keys, and one patient who appears in lis_a only. The graph layer (section 4) resolves the first into one golden patient and leaves the second unmatched — both outcomes are expected and are what the end-to-end test checks for.

2. Conceptual model

A patient is referred for a diagnostic request. Pathology requests produce test results; imaging requests produce a report. Both happen at a site.

3. Logical model (gold layer)

Star schema with two facts sharing four conformed dimensions. Patient names, dates of birth and Medicare numbers stay in silver; gold holds only what analytics needs.

dim_patient.patient_id is the golden id produced by the graph layer (silver.patient.golden_id), not a raw source key — this is what lets fact_pathology_result and fact_imaging_study for the same person join on one patient regardless of which of the three systems the underlying request came from.

4. Solution architecture

Five layers, plus audit running alongside all of them. Each layer has one job.

  • Bronze keeps the source shape so any silver rule can be re run without going back to the systems.
  • Silver conforms each source independently — dates, codes and status become one vocabulary, but a patient from lis_a and the same patient from ris are still two separate rows at this point.
  • Graph resolves identity across systems. This is the prototype's implementation of A1's Recommendation R1: a deterministic Medicare-number-plus-date-of-birth match modelled as a graph, rather than the fuller probabilistic matching a production master patient index would use.
  • Gold is the only layer dashboards read. Identifying details and raw source keys are removed here; patients are addressed only by the graph's golden id.
  • Audit follows the Workshop 6 pattern: one run record per load with counts and status. Validate is the evidence trail for end-to-end testing — orphan-record checks, bronze-to-silver row-count reconciliation, and the raw-vs-golden identifier count that is also the headline MPI dashboard metric (see diagnostics_source_schemas.sql, queries V1–V4).
